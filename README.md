## Game Overview

[Teen Patti Master](https://teenspattimasters.in) is played with a standard international 52-card deck (jokers excluded). Tables seat between 3 and 6 players. The objective of each hand is to hold the highest-ranking three-card combination or to use bluffing and betting strategies to induce opponents to fold before the showdown.

- **Deck:** 52 cards (ranks: 2 to Ace)
- **Hand Size:** 3 cards per participant
- **Deal:** Clockwise, one card at a time, face-down
- **Betting System:** Boot value (initial pot) followed by Chaal (seen) or Blind betting rounds

## Card Sequence Hierarchy

In standard 3-card poker and Teen Patti, hands are evaluated according to the following strict hierarchy:

| Rank | Hand Name | Description | Example |
|:---:|:---|:---|:---|
| **1** | **Trail / Set / Trio** | Three cards of identical face value or number | `A♠ A♥ A♦` (Highest) to `2♠ 2♥ 2♦` (Lowest) |
| **2** | **Pure Sequence** | Three consecutive cards of the exact same suit | `A♥ K♥ Q♥` or `4♠ 5♠ 6♠` |
| **3** | **Normal Sequence** | Three consecutive cards of mixed or different suits | `9♠ 8♥ 7♦` |
| **4** | **Color / Flush** | Three non-consecutive cards sharing the same suit | `K♦ 9♦ 4♦` |
| **5** | **Pair / Double** | Two cards of identical rank plus one unmatched card | `K♠ K♥ 5♣` |
| **6** | **High Card** | Highest single card when no combination is formed | `A♠ J♦ 8♣` |

For a complete breakdown of sequence probabilities and tied-hand tiebreakers, consult the [Teen Patti Sequence and Rules Guide](https://teenspattimasters.in/how-to-play).

## How to Play Teen Patti Master

1. **Boot Round:** Each player contributes a minimum chip amount into the virtual center pot.
2. **The Deal:** Every player receives 3 cards face down.
3. **Blind vs. Seen Play:**
   - **Blind Players:** Bet without looking at their cards. Blind bets require 1x current stake.
   - **Seen Players:** Look at their cards before betting. Seen bets require 2x current stake.
4. **Sideshow / Compromise:** A seen player can request a private sideshow comparison with the previous player. The weaker hand is folded.
5. **Showdown:** When only two active competitors remain, a final showdown reveals cards to determine the winner.

## Popular Game Variations

Teen Patti Master includes multiple game modes for casual and tournament formats:

- **Muflis (Low Card Mode):** Hand rankings are completely inverted. The lowest hand (High Card 5-3-2) wins over a Trio of Aces.
- **AK47:** Aces, Kings, 4s, and 7s act as universal wild jokers to create surprise sets and sequences.
- **Joker Mode:** One random card is drawn at the beginning of the round as the table's wild card.
- **Private Tables:** Enables password-protected private gaming rooms to connect with friends and family.
